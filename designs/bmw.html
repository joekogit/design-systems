ENGINEERING

Balance before power

Weight distribution, steering feel and body control decide whether a car is enjoyable. Power only decides how quickly it gets boring.

50:50 distribution

Achieved by placing the drivetrain rather than by adding ballast to compensate.

Steering feel

Hydraulic assistance retained on the driver's package, because the electric system loses information.

Straight-six

Inherently balanced, revving to seven thousand, with the induction audible rather than synthesised.

Adaptive chassis

Dampers reading the road every two milliseconds, with a comfort setting that is genuinely comfortable.

Electric option

The same chassis philosophy with a rear motor and a 620 kilometre range.

Five-star safety

Structural design rather than assistance systems doing the work in a real impact.

OWNERSHIP

Why keep hydraulic steering?

Because electric assistance filters out surface information the driver uses. It is retained on the driver's package where that feedback is the point.

Is the electric version a different car?

No. Same chassis, same steering geometry, same seating position. The battery placement preserves the weight distribution rather than compromising it.

How long is delivery?

From twelve weeks for a standard specification. Configured orders are given a build slot at the point of order rather than an estimate.

What does servicing include?

Collection from an address of your choosing, a courtesy vehicle, and an itemised report with photographs of any part replaced.

Applying this system

  • The light canvas is the base surface; dark navy appears only inside hero bands. The page rhythm relies on that contrast.
  • Corporate blue is the single primary action colour throughout.
  • The typographic signature is weight 700 display against weight 300 body — a wide gap held consistently.
  • Buttons are rectangular at 0px radius. That corporate dialect is what distinguishes it from the sportier M sub-brand.
  • Model cards run four or five up with no hairline border — just a white plate, the photograph and the title.

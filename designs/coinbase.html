QUESTIONS

What does qualified custody mean?

Assets are held by a regulated custodian in segregated, bankruptcy-remote accounts — legally separate from the company's own balance sheet.

Are fees really shown before I confirm?

Yes, as a currency amount on the confirmation screen, not a percentage you have to compute. The spread is included in that number.

Can I move assets to my own wallet?

Yes, at any time, with the self-custody wallet or any external address. Withdrawal is not gated behind a support conversation.

What happens if the company fails?

Customer assets are segregated and bankruptcy-remote, which means they are not available to creditors. The legal structure is described in full on the custody page.

Applying this system

  • A single blue carries every primary CTA, the wordmark and inline brand links — and it is used scarcely.
  • Display weights stay modest at 400, never heavy. The restraint is the trust signal.
  • Editorial pill geometry throughout: pills for CTAs, circles for asset glyphs, generous rounding on cards. No sharp corners anywhere.
  • The strongest signature is a full-bleed dark hero with floating product-UI cards composited over it.
  • Trading semantics are text colour only — green up, red down — and never a background fill.

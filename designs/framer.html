Questions

Can I export the code?

Yes — clean React components with the styles attached. Most people never do, but the export exists so choosing Loom is not a one-way door.

Is it actually fast, or fast in a demo?

The median published site scores 98 on Lighthouse against real field data, not lab runs. The benchmarks page shows the distribution rather than just the median.

What happens to my site if I stop paying?

The custom domain stops resolving and the site reverts to its Loom subdomain. Nothing is deleted, and reattaching the domain restores it.

Does it handle reduced motion?

Yes, automatically. Scroll and transition effects are suppressed when the visitor's system asks for reduced motion — you do not have to remember to do it.

Applying this system

  • The black canvas carries hero, body, pricing, FAQ and footer alike. There are no light interludes in this system.
  • Display tracking runs aggressively negative — around -5.5px at the largest size. That poster-grade cadence is the typographic signature.
  • The white pill is the only primary CTA shape on the site. Secondary actions are charcoal pills or plain text links.
  • Gradient spotlight cards are individual tiles inside the dark grid — never section backgrounds. Turning one into a band collapses the effect.
  • One chromatic accent is reserved for links, focus and selection. It is never decorative.

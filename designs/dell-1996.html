SUPPORT

How long until it ships?

Five business days from order for a standard configuration. If we miss that, the freight is on us — that is a policy, not a goodwill gesture.

Can I change my configuration after ordering?

Yes, until it enters the build queue, which is usually the following morning. Call the 800 number and the representative can amend it.

What does on-site service cover?

A technician at your location the next business day for the warranty term, with the part in hand. Parts are stocked regionally so the two arrive together.

Is there a return period?

Thirty days, money back, for any reason. You pay the return freight unless the system was misconfigured by us.

Applying this system

  • Every page sits inside a literal black outer frame about 8px thick — the design treats the browser window as a printed picture frame.
  • Flat colour-block ribbon cards tint each product family with its own catalogue colour. No gradients, no shadows, no opacity anywhere.
  • Type is three-tier: a chunky black display for section titles, a bold sans for product rows, and Times for all body copy.
  • Hand-cut GIF stickers overlay the layout — a yellow tab in the corner, angled NEW bursts, round award seals.
  • Dell red is reserved for exactly two things: the main CTA panel and the phone number. It is never decorative.

ENQUIRIES

How are enquiries handled?

Directly by Molsheim rather than through a dealer network. The first conversation is with the commission team, and it establishes what you want from the car before any figure is discussed.

How long is the build?

Six months for Arche and Arche Sport from the confirmation of specification. A Solitaire commission runs to approximately eighteen months.

Is servicing included?

Ten years of scheduled servicing is included, performed by a travelling technician at an address of your choosing anywhere in Europe.

Can the car be driven on the road?

It is homologated for road use in the European Union, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and the United States. Top speed is limited to 380 km/h unless the second key is fitted.

Applying this system

  • Pure black canvas with white type. There is no light mode in this system and no card surface brighter than #141414.
  • Every display headline is uppercase with 2–4px of positive tracking. Body copy stays sentence case at normal tracking — the contrast between the two is the whole voice.
  • There is no accent colour. The single non-monochrome value is a desaturated ice-blue used on inline anchor links, and even that appears rarely.
  • The primary CTA is a transparent pill with a 1px white outline. A filled button would break the restraint the rest of the system depends on.
  • Section rhythm runs to 120px — longer than most marketing sites, because the pages are mostly photography with very little text.

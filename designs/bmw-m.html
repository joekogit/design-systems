Every system on this car exists because the racing programme needed it first.

M XDRIVE

All-wheel drive with a rear-biased default and a genuine rear-only mode for circuit use.

Dampers, steering and differential mapped together rather than configured independently.

OWNERSHIP

Is rear-wheel-drive mode a gimmick?

No. It fully disconnects the front axle rather than reducing torque to it, which is why it is only selectable with stability control off.

Can it be used on track without modification?

Yes. The cooling and brake packages are rated for repeated track use, and doing so does not affect the warranty.

Is the driving course really included?

Two days at a circuit with an instructor per two cars, included with every M purchase. Most of the car's capability is inaccessible otherwise.

What does the CS remove?

Fifty-two kilograms via a carbon roof, bucket seats, a titanium exhaust and lighter wheels. Rear seats are a no-cost option rather than deleted.

Applying this system

  • A near-pure black canvas with white type. The system inverts almost nothing — there is no light marketing surface.
  • Display headlines are uppercase at weight 700; sub-heads stay sentence case at a lighter weight.
  • The M tricolour appears only as 4px stripe dividers and motorsport chrome. It is never a button or a fill.
  • Photography fills entire bands edge to edge, with the car as the subject and UI chrome reduced to small white labels.
  • Buttons are flat with zero radius and uppercase letterspaced labels. That rectangular silhouette is the brand.

QUESTIONS

Is the open-source version limited?

The core of every product is open under MPL 2.0 with no feature gating. The paid tiers add governance, managed state and support rather than capability.

Do the products require each other?

No. Each is usable on its own, and most teams adopt one first. They share a workflow and an identity model when you do run several.

What clouds are supported?

All the major ones, plus three thousand community providers covering everything from DNS registrars to SaaS tools.

How is state handled?

Stored, versioned and locked so two engineers cannot apply conflicting changes. Self-managed users choose their own backend; Cloud manages it for you.

Applying this system

  • The black canvas carries hero, body, pricing, comparison tables and footer alike — there is no light interlude.
  • Each product owns a colour identity with its own button and card variant. Mixing two product colours in one component breaks the system.
  • The typographic voice is the proportional gap: display at tight 1.17–1.21 line-height, body in the same family at a relaxed 1.5–1.7.
  • CTAs sit at 8px, not pills, which keeps the system reading as developer-tool rather than consumer app.
  • Elevation is a charcoal surface lift rather than shadow, and 1px translucent hairlines define cards — felt more than seen.

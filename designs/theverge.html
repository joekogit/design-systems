ABOUT VOLTAGE

How do you score reviews?

Out of ten, in tenths, against the category rather than against the price. A cheap product does not get graded on a curve, and an expensive one does not get credit for costing more.

Do you take review units?

Yes, and we return them. Loaned hardware is disclosed at the top of every review, and no company sees a score or a draft before publication.

Do you run sponsored posts?

No. Advertising appears as advertising, clearly marked and separated from editorial, and no advertiser has ever been given a say in coverage.

Applying this system

  • There is no light mode. The dark canvas is the product; the palette only inverts when a single tile takes a mint, yellow or white fill.
  • Mint and ultraviolet are hazard tape, not background wash. One saturated block per screen region, never two competing.
  • Story tiles are rounded 20–40px and filled edge to edge with colour, stacked on a dashed vertical rule like commits in a log.
  • Depth is entirely flat — 1px hairlines in white, mint or ultraviolet do the work shadows would do elsewhere.
  • Black text appears only on mint, yellow or white tiles. Everywhere else text is white or the #949494 metadata grey.

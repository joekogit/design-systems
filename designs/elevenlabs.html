Questions

Can I clone anyone's voice?

No. Cloning requires verified consent from the speaker, captured through a recorded confirmation phrase. Uploading someone else's audio without it will not produce a clone.

Who owns the audio I generate?

You do, on any paid plan, including commercial use. Free-tier output is licensed for non-commercial use and must credit the platform.

How do the watermarks work?

Every generation carries an inaudible signal that survives normal compression. A public tool checks whether a clip originated here, and it is free to use.

What is the real latency?

Seventy-five milliseconds to first byte on the streaming endpoint, measured from the nearest region. Full-file generation is slower and depends on length.

Applying this system

  • There is no saturated CTA colour. The single primary action is an ink pill, and all the brand voltage comes from the atmospheric gradients.
  • Pastel gradient orbs — mint, peach, lavender, sky, rose — are decoration only. Putting one behind a button breaks the system.
  • Display runs at weight 300 for an editorial magazine voice; body is a sans at 400 with slightly positive tracking.
  • Geometry is soft: pills for CTAs, generous rounding on cards, nothing sharp.
  • Section rhythm is 96px throughout, which is what keeps the page feeling unhurried.

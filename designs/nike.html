NEED TO KNOW

How do they fit?

True to size for most runners, with a slightly wider forefoot than the previous generation. If you sized up half a size in the 03, order your true size in the 04.

Can I return them after running in them?

Yes. Sixty days, worn or unworn, no questions. Take them out on a real run — that is the only way to know.

How long does the foam last?

Rated to four hundred kilometres of rebound on the lab rig. Most runners report the shoe feels flat somewhere between 350 and 500km depending on surface and weight.

Are they legal for competition?

Yes. Stack height and plate count are within World Athletics limits for road racing, and the model is on the approved list.

Applying this system

  • Display type is uppercase at line-height 0.9 and burned directly into full-bleed photography. The photograph is the layout.
  • The palette is pure black, white and one soft grey across roughly 95% of the chrome. Colour arrives only through the imagery.
  • Every CTA, search field, filter chip and badge is a pill. There are no sharp-cornered interactive elements in the system.
  • Product cards have zero radius and zero shadow — they sit directly on a soft-grey swatch, because the photograph is the card.
  • Two-tone CTA hierarchy: black on light, soft-grey on bright. Never both on the same surface.

QUESTIONS

How are errors grouped?

By root cause using the stack trace, not by message text. A single bug producing twenty different messages stays one issue rather than twenty.

Will it be noisy?

Grouping, suppression of known noise and impact-based escalation are the defaults. You can also mute an issue permanently rather than repeatedly dismissing it.

Are the SDKs open source?

Yes, all of them, on GitHub with public issue trackers. The server is source-available and self-hostable.

Does session replay capture sensitive data?

Text and inputs are masked by default. You opt in to unmasking specific elements rather than opting out of capturing everything.

Applying this system

  • Two polarities that never blur: violet midnight for marketing and product pages, white for pricing and dense reference content.
  • The lime highlight is a typographic device, not a swatch — it wraps single words inside a headline like syntax highlighting.
  • Sticker illustrations appear at section junctions and never inside cards. They create rhythm between dense blocks.
  • Uppercase eyebrows and button caps with a slight tracking lift give the system its developer-console cadence.
  • Card chrome follows the canvas: dark sections nest dark cards, light sections nest white ones. Only the polarity flips.

QUESTIONS

How is the AI agent priced?

At a published rate per resolution, charged only when the conversation actually ends without a human. Escalated conversations are not billed as resolutions.

What does it answer from?

Your help centre articles, past resolved conversations and any content you connect. It does not answer from general world knowledge.

What happens when it does not know?

It says so and hands over, passing the full attempt to the agent who picks it up. The handover rate is reported per topic so you can see where your content is thin.

Can we start without the AI agent?

Yes. Essential is a straightforward shared inbox and help centre; the agent is added on Advanced when you want it.

Applying this system

  • The cream canvas is the defining surface — neither white nor grey, deliberately warm.
  • Every section centres a product mockup card; the marketing chrome around it stays minimal.
  • Charcoal is the system primary. Buttons, headlines and body type all sit on it rather than on a brand hue.
  • The orange belongs to the AI product only — its CTA and its badge. Using it decoratively would dilute the signal.
  • Display tracking pulls aggressively negative at large sizes while body copy stays at zero.

QUESTIONS

Is the million-token context real?

It is evaluated at full depth and the per-depth scores are published, including the depths where accuracy dips. Nothing is silently truncated.

Can I use one key for all four lines?

Yes. One key, one billing account, one dashboard across text, music, video and speech.

What are the commercial terms on generated media?

You own the output on any paid tier, including commercial use. Free-tier output is watermarked and non-commercial.

Do you train on my inputs?

No. API inputs and outputs are excluded from training by default, with no setting to remember and no opt-out form to file.

Applying this system

  • A stark monochrome palette is broken open by saturated gradient cards. The monochrome is the default; the colour is the event.
  • Each product line owns a vibrant colour — coral for text, magenta for music, blue for video, orange for speech. Never mix them on one card.
  • Pills everywhere for buttons and tabs. Rectangles appear only inside data tables and dense documentation.
  • Hero type runs at 1.10 line-height with heavy negative tracking for impact.
  • Black promo banners above the nav are reserved for time-bound moments — they are not part of the standing chrome.

Questions we get a lot

Is the free plan genuinely free?

Yes, and permanently. Individuals get unlimited pages and blocks. The paid plans exist for teams that need shared blocks, longer history and administrative controls.

Can I import from other tools?

There are importers for the common suspects — documents, wikis, spreadsheets and Markdown archives — and page structure and links are preserved rather than flattened.

Who owns the content?

You do. Everything can be exported at any time as Markdown, CSV, HTML or PDF, individually or as a full workspace archive.

How does the AI use our data?

Workspace content is used to answer your questions and is not used to train shared models. AI can be disabled workspace-wide by an administrator.

Applying this system

  • The navy hero band is the signature opening move, with the product UI card sitting on it under a deep drop shadow.
  • The purple pill is the primary CTA and appears once per view. Everything else is a text link or a bordered secondary.
  • Buttons are 8px rounded, cards 12px — deliberately not pills. The geometry is sober and editorial.
  • Feature cards draw from a pastel set (peach, rose, mint, lavender, sky, yellow) echoing the product's database property colours.
  • The hero is centred, which sets it apart from the left-aligned norm across B2B SaaS.

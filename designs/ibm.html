QUESTIONS

Can this run entirely on-premise?

Yes. The full platform runs in your own data centre with the same control plane, which is why regulated customers can adopt it at all.

What does AI governance actually cover?

Training-data lineage, model versioning, approval workflow with recorded sign-off, and ongoing drift monitoring — exported as an evidence pack.

Is consulting required?

No, but most large migrations use it. Assessment engagements are fixed-scope so you can find out what is involved before committing.

How is it priced?

By consumption, with committed-use discounts. Enterprise agreements are negotiated, and the rate card is provided before commercial discussions.

Applying this system

  • The marketing chrome is the design system. Buttons are square, inputs are square with a bottom rule, and corners stay at 0px throughout.
  • Light-weight display type — weight 300 at 42–76px — is the typographic signature. Bolding it makes it a different brand.
  • One accent blue carries every link, primary CTA and banner. There is no second brand colour.
  • White canvas, light grey and charcoal cover about 95% of surfaces.
  • The footer inverts to charcoal and is the only dark surface above the page break.

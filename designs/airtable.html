QUESTIONS

Is it really a database?

Yes — linked records, referential integrity, rollups and lookups. It presents as a grid because that is what makes it usable, not because it is a spreadsheet underneath.

Do read-only users cost a seat?

No. You pay for editors. Interface users and commenters do not consume a paid seat, which is what makes rolling it out across a company affordable.

Can I get my data out?

CSV per table, or the API for a full structured export including relations. There is no export fee and no notice period.

What happens at the record limit?

You are warned well before, and the base stays readable rather than locking. Upgrading raises the limit immediately.

Applying this system

  • The hero is a plain white canvas — no gradient, no mesh, no flourish. The strength comes from type and buttons in clean whitespace.
  • The signature button pair is a near-black filled CTA beside a canvas-coloured outline button. They travel together.
  • Brand voltage lives in full-bleed signature colour cards — coral, forest, dark navy — appearing every few screens.
  • Demo grids sit on warm pastel surfaces: peach, mint and cream carrying product UI fragments.
  • The canvas resets to white between every signature surface. That reset is what keeps the colour cards from becoming noise.

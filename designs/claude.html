What it does

A model you can hand real work to

Long documents, messy codebases and questions where being wrong quietly would be expensive.

200k of context

A whole codebase, a full contract set, or a year of meeting notes — held in one conversation without chunking.

Writes real code

Multi-file changes with the reasoning shown, and a willingness to say a plan is wrong before writing it.

Calibrated

Trained to express uncertainty rather than assert fluently. It will tell you when it is guessing.

Safety as design

Constitutional training rather than a filter bolted on afterwards, with the principles published.

Tools and MCP

Calls your functions, reads your systems, and connects to services through an open protocol.

Your data is yours

API inputs and outputs are not used for training. Zero-retention is available on request.

Common questions

Do you train on my data?

Not on API inputs or outputs. Consumer conversations are used to improve the product unless you opt out, and the setting is in preferences rather than buried in a policy page.

What is the context window really?

200,000 tokens, roughly 500 pages, and it holds across the whole conversation rather than degrading after the first few exchanges.

How is safety handled?

Through constitutional training — the model is trained against a published set of principles, rather than having a filter applied to its output afterwards. The principles are public.

Can it browse or run code?

It can call tools you define, including search and code execution, through an open protocol. Nothing runs unless you have wired it up and permitted it.

Start with a real task

Free to try, no card. Give it something you would otherwise put off.

Applying this system

  • The warm cream canvas with warm dark ink is the defining choice. Pure white would make this a different brand entirely.
  • Coral is used scarcely on individual buttons but generously on full-bleed callout cards — two very different doses of the same accent.
  • Slab-serif display at weight 400 with negative tracking, paired with a humanist sans body. That pairing is the literary editorial voice.
  • Product mockups are dark navy cards carrying real code blocks and terminal panels — the brand shows product chrome rather than abstract illustration.
  • Radius is hierarchical: 8px for buttons and inputs, 12px for content cards, 16px for the hero container, pill for badges.

Questions

Is the free plan really free?

Yes, permanently, with three editable boards and unlimited collaborators on them. Older boards become view-only rather than being deleted if you go over.

How many people can be on one board?

Three hundred concurrent editors. Beyond about fifty, follow-me presenting and private mode are what keep it usable rather than chaotic.

Can we export a board?

As PDF, image, or CSV of the notes and their groupings. Boards can also be backed up in bulk from the admin console on Business.

Does it work offline?

Partially. Recently opened boards are cached and edits queue locally, syncing when you reconnect. Creating a new board needs a connection.

Applying this system

  • A stark white canvas with the wordmark in canary yellow is the opening signature. The yellow is brand, not a UI accent.
  • Black pills are the dominant interactive element — the primary CTA is black, never yellow.
  • Feature cards draw from the actual sticky-note palette: yellow, rose, coral, teal and mint.
  • Real board mockups are the feature imagery. Abstract illustration would undercut the whole premise.
  • The footer is a massive dark band with multi-column links — the one heavy surface in an otherwise white system.

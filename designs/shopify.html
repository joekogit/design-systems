Common questions

What are the card fees?

Published per plan and per region — 2.9% plus 30¢ online on Basic, dropping to 2.4% on Advanced. In-person rates are lower and listed on the same page.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes, on every plan. Buy it through us or connect one you already own; the certificate is issued and renewed automatically either way.

What happens to my data if I leave?

Products, customers and orders export as CSV at any time. Theme files are yours to download. There is no export fee and no notice period.

Do I need a developer?

No, for a standard store — themes are edited visually. A developer helps for bespoke checkout logic or a custom storefront, and there is a full API for both.

Applying this system

  • Two canvases that never blend: cinematic night for marketing storytelling, cream and light for transactional surfaces.
  • The pill is the only button shape across both tracks. Rounded rectangles do not exist for buttons in this system.
  • Thin-weight (330) display type is the signature — 96px at weight 330 is the brand's loudest visual, and bolding it destroys the effect.
  • Aloe and pistachio greens belong to the light track only, where they signal commerce and transactional success.
  • Photography is full-bleed and edge-to-edge on the cinematic track, never inset into cards. Merchants do the visual lifting that gradients would do elsewhere.

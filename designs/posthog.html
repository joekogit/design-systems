Why one platform

The tools stop disagreeing

Four vendors means four definitions of an active user and four SDKs racing on page load. One event stream means the funnel, the replay and the flag are all describing the same session.

  • One SDK and one event definition
  • Replay linked from any funnel step
  • Flags and experiments share cohorts

Talk to us

Questions

Is the free tier a trial?

No. A million events a month, permanently, across all six products. Plenty of small products never pay anything.

Do you charge per seat?

No. Invite your whole company; you pay for events and replays, not for people looking at them.

Can I self-host?

Yes, the entire platform, not an open-core subset. Docker Compose for small deployments and Kubernetes charts for larger ones.

Where is my data stored?

In the cloud region you pick, EU or US, and it never moves between them. Self-hosting keeps it entirely on your own infrastructure.

Applying this system

  • The warm cream canvas runs end to end with no surface alternation — the page is one continuous sheet.
  • A single yellow-orange pill with deep olive text is the only saturated colour in the system.
  • One typeface across every text role, at five weights. There is no second family.
  • Hand-drawn mascots scattered through the layout are the entire decorative system — no gradients, no mesh, no atmospherics.
  • Pastel callout banners with soft tinted side rails break up long documentation copy for tips and warnings.

Common questions

Are fees included in the price I see?

Yes. Cleaning and service fees are in the headline number from the first search result onwards, so the total at checkout is the number you have been looking at all along.

What is the cancellation policy?

It is set per listing and shown as a filter before you search. Flexible allows free cancellation up to 24 hours before check-in; the stricter tiers are labelled plainly on the listing.

How does damage protection work?

Hosts are covered up to three million per booking. Claims are filed through the resolution centre with photographs, and the guest is contacted before anything is charged.

When do hosts get paid?

The morning after check-in, to the account on file. Longer stays are paid monthly rather than in a lump at the end.

Applying this system

  • One accent — Rausch — carries every primary CTA, the search orb and the save state. Most of the page is white and ink with one or two Rausch moments.
  • Display weights sit at 500–700 and body at 400. The modest weight is deliberate: the system trusts photography for visual heft.
  • The global search is a fully-rounded white pill divided by 1px hairlines into segments, terminated by a circular accent orb.
  • Property cards are photo-first — a clipped aspect-ratio image, a floating badge top-left, a heart top-right, then four or five lines of meta.
  • Elevation caps at a single shadow tier used on hover-floated cards and dropdowns. Footer and language menus are plain text columns with no card surface at all.

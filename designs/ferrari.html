Engineering

BUILT AROUND THE ENGINE

Everything else in the car exists to serve the twelve cylinders behind the driver.

V12, unassisted

Six and a half litres, no turbochargers, no electrical augmentation. The response is immediate because there is nothing in between.

9,500rpm

A flat-plane crank and titanium rods let the engine run past nine thousand without drama, which is where it does its best work.

Active aerodynamics

A rear surface that reconfigures across three states, generating 390kg of downforce at maximum without a fixed wing.

1,380kg dry

A carbon monocoque, carbon body panels and a titanium exhaust. Weight was removed, not offset.

Manual gearbox

Six ratios, an open gate, and a mechanical linkage. Offered because a small number of owners still ask for it.

Homologated

Developed alongside the racing programme and validated over 18,000km at Fiorano and the Nordschleife.

Racing

FIORANO, EVERY WEEK

The road car and the race car are developed on the same circuit by the same engineers. Suspension geometry, aerodynamic balance and brake cooling all arrived on the road car because they were needed first on the track.

  • 18,000km of development at Fiorano
  • Shared aerodynamic platform with the competition car
  • Brake package homologated for endurance racing

OWNERSHIP QUESTIONS

How is allocation decided?

By the dealer network, in consultation with Maranello. Ownership history is the primary consideration, and the list for the Tipo 12 closed within eleven days of the announcement.

Is the manual gearbox a delete option?

It is a separate specification rather than a deletion, and it is only offered on the Assetto. It carries no price difference and a slightly longer build slot.

What is included in maintenance?

Seven years of scheduled servicing including fluids, filters, brake wear and collection from an address of your choosing. Consumables from track use are not included.

Can the car be used on track?

It is homologated for road use and developed on circuit. Track use does not affect the warranty, though the tyre and brake packages are treated as consumables.

THE LAST OF THE LINE

599 cars, and no successor planned. Speak to your dealer about allocation.

Applying this system

  • The canvas is near-black (#181818), never pure black. White bands appear only inside editorial contexts.
  • Rosso Corsa is a single accent used scarcely — the CTA, the marque device, a race-position highlight. It never becomes a background.
  • Display weight stays at 500. Nothing in this system is bold; the scale carries the emphasis instead.
  • Every corner is square. Zero radius on CTAs, cards and bands is the luxury-automotive precision cue.
  • Depth is hairlines and photographic contrast. There is no drop-shadow tier anywhere in the system.

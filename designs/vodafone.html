HELP

Is the price really fixed?

Yes, for the contract term, written into the terms rather than promised in marketing. There is no annual inflation-linked increase.

What does unlimited actually mean?

No data cap and no speed reduction after a threshold. Traffic management applies only to the two lower tiers and is described in the plan detail.

How accurate is the coverage map?

It is built from measured signal reports rather than propagation modelling, and the methodology is published. Where confidence is low, the map says so.

Can I leave if it is bad?

Within thirty days, yes, for any reason, with the handset returned. After that, broadband customers can leave without penalty if the guaranteed minimum speed is not met.

Applying this system

  • Massive uppercase display at weight 800 is the signature; weight-300 siblings handle the calmer secondary moments.
  • One red carries every CTA — pill-filled for primary, pill-outline for secondary. There is no third button variant.
  • The speechmark orb is the only decorative chrome that is not a call to action, and it anchors the centre of the page.
  • Pill geometry on every interactive shape: 60px on buttons, 32px on inline badges, with card chrome staying at 6px.
  • The page rhythm is two bands — dark hero, light content. There are no mid-tone greys; contrast does the elevation.

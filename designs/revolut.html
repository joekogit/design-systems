QUESTIONS

What is the real exchange rate?

The interbank rate, shown alongside our markup as a separate figure. Above your fee-free allowance the markup applies; below it, it does not.

Is my money protected?

Deposits are held at partner banks under the relevant protection scheme in your country, and the specific scheme and limit are stated on the account page.

What happens at the weekend?

Currency markets close, so a small weekend surcharge applies on exchanges. It is shown before you confirm rather than discovered on the statement.

Can I use it as my main account?

Yes — salary can be paid in, direct debits are supported, and the account details are your own rather than pooled.

Applying this system

  • Two canvas modes switched in full-bleed bands: true black for storytelling, white for browsing. Never blended in one section.
  • Display type runs tight — line-height 1.0 with heavy negative tracking at large sizes.
  • The actual primary CTA is a white pill with black text on the dark canvas, deliberately the brightest pixel. The brand violet is for featured plan cards and secondary actions.
  • Eight saturated accents live inside product mockups and illustrations only. None of them is ever a button surface.
  • All buttons are pills, content cards take a generous 20px, and inputs and chips step down to 12px.

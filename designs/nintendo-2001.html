SUPPORT

What is in the box?

The console, one controller, an AV cable, the power supply and the printed manual. Memory cards and additional controllers are sold separately.

Will my old cartridges work?

No. The Polygon 64 uses a new cartridge format with a different pin count and a physically different slot, so previous-generation games will not fit.

How many players is it?

Four, out of the box, with four ports on the front of the console. No adapter is required, though not every title supports four players.

What does the expansion bay do?

The memory expansion module doubles the working RAM and enables a high-resolution display mode on titles that support it. It is included in the Collector bundle.

Applying this system

  • Every region is a bevelled metal plate: a light top edge, a chrome-indigo shadow line below. The page is assembled from panels rather than laid out on a grid.
  • Chamfered (cut-corner) geometry on the largest modules; most chrome is sharp-edged, with rounding reserved for the logo pill and circular badges.
  • A carbon-navy command layer with halftone texture carries the top nav, the right rail and the footer.
  • Warmth is rationed as directional signal only — gold for menu words, amber for utility chips, orange for every forward or submit cue.
  • Density is the point. Minimal whitespace, packed modular panels, and a hero field tinted per section.

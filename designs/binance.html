QUESTIONS

How is proof of reserves verified?

Balances are hashed into a Merkle tree audited quarterly against on-chain holdings, with a tool that lets you confirm your own balance is included.

What are maker rebates?

At VIP 1 and above the maker fee is negative — you are paid for adding liquidity. The full schedule is published rather than negotiated privately.

Where are assets held?

About ninety-five percent offline in geographically separated vaults with multi-party signing; the rest sits in hot wallets sized for expected withdrawals.

Is there an API testnet?

Yes, mirroring production including the awkward error codes. Rate limits are documented per endpoint rather than discovered under load.

Applying this system

  • A single yellow does all the brand voltage — CTAs, headlines, the mark and badges. Scarcely on dark, ubiquitously on transactional dialogs.
  • Numbers and prices use a separate face from body text so columns of figures align. That tabular consistency is non-negotiable in this system.
  • Marketing pages default dark and transactional pages flip light. Yellow CTAs and trading green/red are shared across both.
  • The homepage closes with a light footer beneath a dark body — a deliberate inversion that visually shuts the page.
  • Trading semantics are text colour only: green up, red down, never as badge backgrounds. Surfaces stay flat with no gradients.

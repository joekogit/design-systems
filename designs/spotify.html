Questions

What happens if I cancel?

The account reverts to the free tier at the end of the billing period. Playlists, saved albums and listening history are all retained, and downloads simply become unavailable until you resubscribe.

Does lossless cost extra?

No. Every Premium tier plays 24-bit audio wherever a lossless master exists. There is no separate hi-fi add-on.

How many devices can I use?

Sign in on as many devices as you like; play on one at a time. Offline downloads are limited to five devices and ten thousand tracks each.

How are artists paid?

Under a user-centric model, your subscription is split between the artists you actually listened to that month, rather than pooled and divided by total platform share.

Applying this system

  • The UI is achromatic by design — album art is the colour source. Green is functional (play, active, CTA) and never decorative.
  • Pill-and-circle geometry throughout: full-radius buttons, 50% circular play controls, 500px search pills. There are no sharp corners.
  • Button labels are uppercase with 1.4–2px tracking, which reads as a systematic label voice distinct from content text.
  • Type is a bold/regular binary — 700 or 400, with 600 used sparingly. Hierarchy comes from weight, not size.
  • The reference documents a compact 10–24px app UI scale; the display tier here extends it upward for a marketing surface, keeping the same weight logic.

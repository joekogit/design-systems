Frequently asked

Will my existing lenses fit?

Every lens made for the previous mount fits without an adapter and retains autofocus and stabilisation. Third-party lenses on the same mount are supported but not calibrated at the factory.

What is included in the box?

The body, a braided charge cable, the strap and the printed guide. A power adapter is sold separately, which keeps roughly 600 tonnes of material out of the supply chain each year.

How does trade-in work?

Answer four questions about the camera you own, receive an estimate immediately, and the credit is applied at checkout. The value is confirmed when the device arrives.

Is it repairable?

Yes. The battery, sensor assembly, card door and rear screen are all serviceable modules, and the parts and manuals are sold to independent repair shops.

Applying this system

  • The section rhythm is the layout: light hero → dark product tile → light utility tile → dark tile → parchment footer. The colour change is the divider; do not add rules between sections.
  • One blue accent carries every interactive element. There is no second brand colour anywhere in the system.
  • Two button grammars only — small blue pills for calls to action, compact rounded rectangles for utility.
  • Exactly one drop shadow exists in the whole system, and it is reserved for a product image that needs to breathe.
  • Negative tracking at display sizes produces the tight headline feel; body text stays at normal tracking.

Rewards questions

Do stars expire?

Stars stay on the account for twelve months from the day they are earned. The app shows the expiry date for each batch and reminds you before any batch lapses.

Can I earn stars on gift card purchases?

You earn stars when you pay with a registered gift card, not when you buy one. That avoids earning twice on the same money.

Does bring-your-own-cup work with order ahead?

Yes. Select it in the app when ordering and hand the cup over at collection. The discount and the double stars apply the same way.

What happens to my stars if I do not visit for a while?

Nothing for twelve months. Individual star batches expire on their own dates rather than the whole balance being wiped after a period of inactivity.

Applying this system

  • There is no single 'brand green'. Four greens map to four surface roles — Starbucks for headings, Accent for filled CTAs, House for dark bands and footer, Uplift for decorative moments.
  • Gold is Rewards-status ceremony only. Using it as a general accent is the fastest way to break this system.
  • The canvas is warm cream, never cold white — it references café materials, and every neutral is tinted to match.
  • Buttons are full pills at 50px radius, universally. The signature micro-interaction is scale(0.95) on active press.
  • Cards sit at 12px with whisper-soft shadows: flat, plus a hint of lift, never a floating panel.

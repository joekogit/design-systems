Common questions

How does the migration work?

Import from Jira, Asana, GitHub Issues or a CSV. Identifiers, comments, attachments and status history are preserved, and a dry run shows exactly what will be created before anything is written.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The workspace is held locally and reconciled when the connection returns, so reading and writing continue on a plane or a bad hotel network.

Can we self-host?

Self-hosting is available on Enterprise. Most teams choose the managed service with data residency in their region instead.

What happens when a member leaves?

Billing is based on members active in the period, so removing someone stops the charge on the next invoice with no seat reconciliation.

Applying this system

  • The canvas is the whitespace. Separate sections by lifting content onto surface-1 panels rather than by adding gaps.
  • Use the lavender accent on the brand mark, focus rings and one CTA per view. A second accent colour breaks the system.
  • Depth comes from the surface ladder plus a hairline border — resist drop shadows on the dark canvas.
  • Display tracking runs aggressively negative (about -4% of the size); the eyebrow deliberately runs positive to read as taxonomy.
  • Substitute Inter or Geist for the proprietary Linear cut, and JetBrains Mono for Linear Mono.

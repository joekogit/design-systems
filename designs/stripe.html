Questions about pricing

Are there any setup or monthly fees?

No. Integrated pricing is purely per successful transaction, so an account that processes nothing in a month is charged nothing that month.

How are refunds and disputes handled?

Refunding a charge returns the full amount to the customer; the original processing fee is not returned. Disputes carry a separate fee that is reversed automatically when the dispute is resolved in your favour.

Do you support interchange-plus?

Yes, on Scale and Enterprise. Interchange, scheme fees and the margin are itemised separately on every balance transaction.

When do payouts arrive?

On a rolling two-day schedule by default, configurable to daily, weekly or monthly, with instant payouts available to supported debit cards.

Applying this system

  • The gradient mesh belongs in the upper third of the page only — cream, orange, lavender, indigo and ruby washed horizontally. It never repeats lower down.
  • Exactly one filled indigo pill per view. Every other action is a ghost link with a chevron.
  • Display type sits at weight 300 with negative tracking; the lightness is the brand, so resist bolding headlines.
  • Any cell containing money uses tabular figures (font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums). This is the quiet financial-data signal.
  • Cream bands break long white stretches without introducing a new hue.

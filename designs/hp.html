SUPPORT

Can I repair it myself?

Yes. Standard screws, a replaceable battery, and service manuals published free. Self-repair does not void the warranty on unrelated components.

How long will parts be available?

Five years from the end of sale, committed in writing rather than offered as a best effort.

Is third-party ink allowed?

Yes. Cartridges are not region-locked and third-party ink does not disable the printer. That policy is published and we do not change it by firmware update.

What is the real battery life?

Fifteen hours on mixed use at 150 nits — office work, browsing and video calls. The test methodology is published alongside the figure.

Applying this system

  • Pure white with deep ink running every body surface; light fog bands alternate to set the section rhythm.
  • The electric blue is the lone CTA fill and link colour, and it appears at most twice per viewport.
  • One bespoke face covers display, body, button and caption at four weights.
  • Cards round at 16px while buttons stay tight at 4px with capitalised labels — the gap between those radii is deliberate.
  • Geometric blue chevrons, rectangles cut at 45 degrees, frame the hero photography and echo the wordmark.

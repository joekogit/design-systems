QUESTIONS

What happens to messages older than 90 days on Free?

They are hidden rather than deleted, and become visible again if you upgrade. Nothing is destroyed by staying on the free plan.

Do you charge for inactive members?

No. Billing is based on members active in the period, and credit is applied automatically for anyone who was not.

Can we work with people outside the company?

Yes, through shared channels. Both sides stay in their own workspace with their own policies, which is what makes it usable across organisations.

Where is our data stored?

In the region you select, with residency available on Business+ for organisations that require it contractually.

Free to start, and the first channel takes about a minute.

Applying this system

  • A single aubergine is reused across CTAs, the featured pricing tier, the footer band and the wordmark — chromatic monotheism.
  • The hero canvas is cream-lavender with a diffused pastel mesh behind it, and product UI composited on top of the gradient, never inside it.
  • Pills carry 28–30px of horizontal padding — deliberately over-padded by SaaS-default standards.
  • Display sizes take tight negative tracking for editorial density.
  • Blue inline links are the only non-aubergine chromatic accent anywhere in body type.
